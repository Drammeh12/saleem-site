About me

Technology should not just exist. It should solve problems.

I'm a young technology entrepreneur, software developer, and cybersecurity enthusiast with a vision to build companies that solve real problems across Africa and beyond. As Founder & CEO of Bash Lucent, I'm building software and digital infrastructure for individuals, students, and businesses.

My interests span software engineering, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and financial technology, and how these can work together to create useful, scalable solutions. My approach to learning is practical: study the fundamentals, experiment in authorised environments, build projects, find problems, and keep improving.

What I build

A connected portfolio of products, under Bash Lucent.

Rather than one project, I'm building a company, and a family of products that each solve a real, daily problem.

Cloud infrastructure

Bash Cloud

A platform that takes code from GitHub to a live, secure web address: hosting, databases, storage, and uptime, built for African developers. It runs on its own domain and is deployed on real server infrastructure.

Financial technology

ClearKobo

Reconciliation for businesses: it compares payments against bank statements and processors, flags missing settlements and duplicate charges, and makes financial records agree, with AI-assisted analysis.

Education network

Naija Campus

A student-focused platform to connect academic communities, discover opportunities, and learn. It starts with Nigerian students and grows into a wider African academic network, with an AI study assistant.
